synthesized sound effects (Web Audio oscillators, no audio files).

## Inspiration

- Layout and presentation ideas from curated design galleries such as
  [Inspora](https://www.inspora.design/): visuals first, generous whitespace, pill filters.
- Pacing notes from Jake Gordon’s write-up
  [“Gameplay in Breakout”](https://jakesgordon.com/writing/gameplay-in-breakout/).
- README structure inspired by the direct presentation of
  [gabrielecirulli/2048](https://github.com/gabrielecirulli/2048).

No code, art or level from these references is reused.
